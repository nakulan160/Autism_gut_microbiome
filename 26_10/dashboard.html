How to read these plots

  • Lower validation MSE means smaller mean squared errors on the fixed masked validation task.
  • Training improving while validation worsens can indicate overfitting; inspect the full trend.
  • Scatter points close to the dashed diagonal indicate accurate abundance predictions.
  • Residual is prediction minus target. Positive values mean overprediction; negative values mean underprediction.
  • Prediction plots use at most 10,000 validation masked pairs in fixed order. All validation samples contribute to loss.
  • Run-level random validation does not establish donor-independent or cross-study generalization.
